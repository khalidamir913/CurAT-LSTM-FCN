# CurAT-LSTM-FCN: Curriculum Adversarial Training for Time Series Classification

Code, results and analysis for the manuscript **"Adversarial Robustness in Time Series Classification: Curriculum Training, Recurrent Resilience, and a Multi-Architecture Benchmark"**.

The repository contains
* the training and white-box attack pipeline for ResNet, FCN, LSTM, LSTM-FCN (Vanilla) and **CurAT-LSTM-FCN**, a three-phase curriculum adversarial-training procedure for LSTM-FCN;
* the per-model, per-seed results on 15 UCR datasets (`results_multiseed.xlsx`);
* the code that turns those results into every table, statistic and data-driven figure of the paper.

## Repository layout

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `01_pipeline.ipynb` | Trains the five models on **one** UCR dataset, generates FGSM / BIM / PGD / C&W adversarial test sets, evaluates, and writes `<DATASET>_multiseed_records.xlsx`. Run once per dataset (GPU recommended). |
| `merge_records.py` | Merges the 15 per-dataset result files into `results_multiseed.xlsx`, removes duplicate rows, and validates the file. |
| `results_multiseed.xlsx` | Master results: 165 rows = 15 datasets × (3 baselines + 2 models × 4 seeds). Accuracies are fractions in [0, 1]. |
| `02_analysis.ipynb` | Reproduces Tables 1, 3–7, the appendix table, all statistical tests, and Figures 3–10 from `results_multiseed.xlsx` only (no GPU, no raw data). |
| `requirements.txt` | Python dependencies. |

Figures 1 and 2 are drawings and are not generated by code.

## Method in brief

* **Models.** ResNet, FCN and a two-layer LSTM are trained once per dataset. LSTM-FCN Vanilla and CurAT-LSTM-FCN are trained once per seed (seeds 0–3). CurAT-LSTM-FCN is warm-started from the Vanilla model of the same seed.
* **Perturbation budget.** ε(T) = clip(0.1·√(24/T), 0.01, 0.10), where T is the series length and 24 is the shortest series in the benchmark (MelbournePedestrian).
* **Curriculum (CurAT).** Phase 1: clean training for W = min(750, 0.5·E) epochs. Phase 2: the adversarial share of each minibatch ramps linearly from 0 to MAX_ADV (FGSM examples) until epoch R = min(900, 0.75·E). Phase 3: share MAX_ADV, alternating FGSM and BIM epochs (BIM_ITER iterations, step ε/BIM_ITER). MAX_ADV = clip(0.70 − 0.15·ln(1 + d), 0.35, 0.70) with d = ln(1 + C)·√(T/N); BIM_ITER = clip(round(5 + 5(T − 200)/1600), 5, 10). A CollapseGuard stops training if validation accuracy stays near chance.
* **Evaluation attacks (all white-box, against each model's own gradients).** FGSM (1 step); BIM (100 iterations, step ε/10); PGD (100 iterations, step ε/10, random start); C&W-L2 (fixed c = 1, κ = 0, 200 Adam iterations, learning rate 0.01, final iterate, no search over c). FGSM/BIM/PGD are bounded by the L∞ budget ε; C&W minimises an L2 penalty and is not bounded by ε, so its accuracies are not directly comparable to the other three.
* **Statistics.** The unit of analysis is the dataset (n = 15) after averaging the seeded models over seeds: paired Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction, Friedman test, Cliff's δ, matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation, Spearman correlation with series length, and a paired bootstrap on the coefficient of variation.

## Reproducing the results

**A. Only the tables, statistics and figures (minutes, CPU).**
1. `pip install -r requirements.txt`
2. Put `results_multiseed.xlsx` next to `02_analysis.ipynb` and run all cells. Outputs go to `outputs/figures` (PNG, SVG, PDF) and `outputs/tables` (CSV and one Excel workbook).
   Figures 3 and 9 additionally need the ECG5000 training histories (`*_history.json`); set `HIST_DIR` in the configuration cell, otherwise those two cells are skipped.

**B. The full pipeline (GPU, hours per dataset).**
1. Download the datasets from the [UCR Time Series Archive](https://www.cs.ucr.edu/~eamonn/time_series_data_2018/) and place `<DATASET>_TRAIN.txt` and `<DATASET>_TEST.txt` in `My Drive/` (Google Colab) or adapt `DRIVE_ROOT` in the configuration cell.
2. Open `01_pipeline.ipynb` in Colab with a GPU runtime, set `DATASET_NAME`, and run the cells top to bottom. The four seed cells (Section 14) are independent; training is resumable and finished models are re-loaded instead of re-trained.
3. Repeat for each of the 15 datasets: ChlorineConcentration, ECG5000, EthanolLevel, FaceAll, FiftyWords, FordA, FordB, MelbournePedestrian, MixedShapesRegularTrain, NonInvasiveFetalECGThorax1, PhalangesOutlinesCorrect, SemgHandMovementCh2, ShapesAll, SwedishLeaf, TwoPatterns.
4. Collect the 15 `<DATASET>_multiseed_records.xlsx` files in one folder and run
   `python merge_records.py <folder> -o results_multiseed.xlsx`
   (use `python merge_records.py --check results_multiseed.xlsx` to validate an existing file).

## Main result (mean over 15 datasets, accuracy in %)

| Model | Clean | FGSM | BIM | PGD | C&W | Mean-attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ResNet | 86.99 | 42.86 | 32.17 | 31.89 | 8.01 | 28.73 |
| FCN | 85.17 | 37.17 | 29.73 | 29.32 | 13.80 | 27.50 |
| LSTM | 71.45 | 48.80 | 41.46 | 41.57 | 24.09 | 38.98 |
| LSTM-FCN Vanilla | 86.52 | 39.67 | 29.40 | 29.19 | 15.41 | 28.42 |
| **CurAT-LSTM-FCN** | 80.72 | **68.58** | **57.38** | **57.65** | 12.56 | **49.04** |

CurAT-LSTM-FCN has the higher mean-attack accuracy than LSTM-FCN Vanilla on 13 of 15 datasets (mean gain +20.62 points; Friedman average rank 1.47 vs 4.00) at a clean-accuracy cost of 5.81 points. It does not improve C&W accuracy on average (12.56% vs 15.41%).

## Limitations and notes

* ResNet, FCN and LSTM are single runs and are not seeded; only LSTM-FCN Vanilla and CurAT-LSTM-FCN use four fixed seeds.
* Checkpoints are selected on the test split: the best validation accuracy for the standard models, and for CurAT-LSTM-FCN the best clean accuracy in Phase 1 followed by the best FGSM accuracy at ε in Phases 2–3. FGSM results for CurAT-LSTM-FCN may therefore be optimistic.
* C&W uses a fixed trade-off constant c = 1 and returns the final iterate rather than the smallest successful perturbation, so it is a weaker attack than the original C&W with a search over c.
* The LSTM branch of LSTM-FCN uses dimension shuffle (the LSTM sees one time step with T features), as in the original LSTM-FCN.
* Gradient masking was not specifically tested for the LSTM baseline.
